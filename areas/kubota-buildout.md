# Kubota — Equipment and Build-Out

The truck itself, specified. Four sides, each carrying one function;
one equipment list, everything it needs to carry to run a full
rehearsal.

## Four sides

- **Rear** — La Promessa's lavagna (chalkboard) and the bar for Il
  Brindisi.
- **Front** — Panem et Circenses' painted backdrop, permanently
  mounted, trompe l'oeil with the heraldic "P"/"C" shield worked into
  it. See panem-et-circenses.md.
- **Side 1** — the chain object's plinth and its 10×10 back panel. See
  chain-barter.md.
- **Side 2** — BosaTAZ's QR codes panel. See bosataz-handover.md and
  bosataz-explainer.md.

Rear and front are opposite each other; side 1 and side 2 are opposite
each other. BosaTAZ's own hardware (Pi Zero, SD card, USB data key)
lives inside the vehicle box, not on any external side.

## Equipment list

- Chalk
- Keg of water
- Beer chiller
- Glasses
- Mat, for unwanted objects (L'Usato)
- Box of unwanted objects
- Cordless hot glue gun
- Battery box (BosaTAZ power)
- Buckets
- Snacks
- Bell (Remembrancer, end of Act Three)
- "R" baseball cap (Remembrancer)
- Barista's apron
- First aid kit
- Folding chair, for Il Suonatore — the entire prop list. No
  instrument case, no stand, nothing further. Position it wherever
  they happen to be playing that day; job done.

**Panem et Circenses props**, separate from the above but riding with
the truck given the backdrop's now fixed to it: juggling balls, a
small table, a simple hat, a waistcoat. See panem-et-circenses.md for
the full staging.

## Not on this list

Rosemary and myrtle branches for the scent moment — cut. That optional
note in 07-staging-la-prova.md no longer applies; this truck carries
no fire-dependent equipment.
