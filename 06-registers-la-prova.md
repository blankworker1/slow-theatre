# La Prova: Registers

*Private working document. Not for participants, not for publication. This is the score — the thing that lets one person hold the whole composition in view, without conducting it.*

---

## Purpose

La Prova's script does not change. This document sits *above* it, not inside it. It exists so that, over many separate performance days, it's possible to tell whether the piece is still in register — whether the layers overlaying La Prova are coherent — or whether something is starting to crowd, collide, or read as scripted when it should read as real.

No participant, performer, or layer needs to see this. Most won't know it exists. Its value is entirely in staying private: it's what lets ambiguity be deliberate rather than merely confused.

---

## The core constraint set

Coherence comes from a small, fixed vocabulary — not from synchronization, not from live direction, not from writing more instructions. Every layer, whether deliberately built or genuinely unconnected, is implicitly working within these:

**1. Place**
La Prova holds the centre of the square and its three Acts. Layers occupy the periphery, or registers that do not overlap La Prova's active space. A layer that wants the same space at the same time as an Act is not a separate layer — it's a collision.

**2. Exchange**
Value-for-value only. No cash, no external valuation, no fixed pricing. Any layer that transacts, in any form, inherits this constraint automatically — whether or not it knows it's inheriting anything. A layer that introduces money or fixed price has left the composition.

**3. Recurrence**
Things return, unexplained, at their own pace. No layer needs to time itself against another, or against La Prova. Recurrence is what makes a layer legible over time without ever being announced.

**4. Silence**
Silence is a valid, present voice — not an absence. A layer that doesn't show up on a given day is not a failure of the composition; it's simply not sounding that week. Nothing needs to fill the gap.

---

## What counts as "in register"

A layer is in register if it is either:

- **Silent and fixed** — a consistent, minimal presence (posture, position, absence of interaction) repeated without variation; or
- **Genuinely independent** — something already happening on its own terms, unconnected to La Prova, that simply happens to share place and the no-cash constraint.

The failure mode sits between these two: a layer that is scripted enough to feel constructed, but connected enough to blur into La Prova's own mechanics. Anything drifting toward that middle needs to be pulled back toward one pole or the other — made more silent, or left more alone.

---

## Debrief questions (after each performance day)

- Did any layer share the same space and time as an Act itself, rather than sitting alongside it?
  
- Did any layer's exchange involve cash, fixed pricing, or external valuation?
  
- Did two layers brush against each other in a way that read as coincidence, or in a way that read as staged?
  
- Did an unconnected layer accidentally appear scripted — or did a deliberate layer accidentally appear too loose, too "real," in a way that risked confusion rather than depth?
  
- Did silence (a layer's absence) get treated as a gap that needed filling, by anyone?
  
- Is there a layer currently occupying a register that La Prova itself needs?

---

## What this document is not

- It is not the public ground-rules document (legal reassurance, participant-facing, states permissions).
- It is not a script, and it adds no new lines, roles, or acts to La Prova.
- It is not shown to performers, layers, or the comune.
- It does not need to stay complete or tidy — it's a working score, revised privately as the composition is heard over more days.
