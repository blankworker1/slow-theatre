# Il Custode

**What the role is.** Not assigned, not chosen, not applied for. Il Custode
is whoever happens to be holding the chain object the moment the
Remembrancer's bell ends Act Three. The role exists for exactly one
rehearsal-to-rehearsal interval, then passes on at the next rehearsal's
first trade.

**The one responsibility.** Bring the object to the next rehearsal, and
place it back on its permanent plinth — no ceremony, just continuity.
Nothing else is expected. Il Custode isn't required to protect the object
beyond ordinary care, isn't required to explain its history to anyone who
asks — though nothing stops them — and holds no special standing beyond
this single task.

**What Il Custode is not.** Not an owner. The object is only ever fully
given away once, at the very start, by the first Donatore — every trade
after that moves custody, not property. Il Custode holds what the whole
Node owns collectively, for one interval, and hands that custody onward the
moment the object goes back into play.

**The chain mechanic, for context.**

- A first Donatore places an object on its own permanent plinth — separate
  from the Gallery's ordinary pedestals, which turn over completely each
  rehearsal. This one is always there, and never empty.
- The Remembrancer records the object's form.
- From that point, any Barterer may trade something for it, any number of
  times, for as long as that day's barter window stays open. No cap on how
  many times it changes hands in one rehearsal.
- Each trade is recorded by the Remembrancer the same way.
- The object is frozen the moment the Remembrancer's bell ends Act Three —
  not Act Two. Whoever holds it at that exact instant becomes Il Custode.
- Before the closing photograph, the Remembrancer chalks one additional
  line naming the custodian and the object — see roles/remembrancer.md for
  the exact form this takes.
- Il Custode brings the object to the next rehearsal and places it back on
  the plinth, opening it to trading again.
