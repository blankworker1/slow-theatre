# What is Lo Spuntino?

Lo Spuntino is the hospitality of a shared table. Nothing more complicated than that.

*Spuntino* — Italian for a snack, a light informal meal — is a familiar rural
tradition: an outdoor gathering, food shared, no invitation needed beyond
"come." Nobody needs to explain what it is. It's simply the name for
something people already know how to do.

## How it works

People bring food and drink and place it on the table. Others take from
it. Nobody keeps track of whose bottle became whose glass, and nobody
needs to. There's no pairing, no menu, no payment — things appear,
and get eaten. That's the whole mechanism.

## Why it matters

Most exchanges, even generous ones, still involve two people who know
who they're dealing with. The spuntino table removes even that. It isn't
an exchange between two people at all — it's a table that belongs to
everyone and no one at once.

## The crossover

This isn't a theatrical idea borrowed from somewhere else and adapted for
a performance. It's a genuine Slow Food practice — the same shared table
Slow Food has always pointed to as its clearest idea, eaten slowly, judged
by nobody but the people at it. Lo Spuntino simply is that table. It's the
one point where Slow Food and Slow Theatre stop being two separate things
and turn out to be doing the same thing at once.
