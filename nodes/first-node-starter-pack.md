# First Node Starter Pack

*A practical companion, not a new layer of philosophy — everything here is
already decided elsewhere in the project; this just consolidates it in one
place for a Node putting on its first performance. Worked example
throughout: BosaNode, staging La Prova in Bosa.*

---

## Precursor stage: site, not equipment

Lo Spuntino, run alone, needs almost none of the equipment below — no
truck, no chalkboard, no bell. What it needs is the right place. The
event and the people are transient; the location should be permanent,
chosen once and returned to rather than found fresh each time.

- **Genuinely public land — an Area Picnic Pubblica, not a private
  campagna.** On public land, nobody is merely choosing not to
  exclude; structurally, nobody can. See bosataz-boundaries.md's
  physical boundary section for the fuller reasoning.
- **A large table and BBQ area — ideally permanent stone
  construction, not brought and packed away each time.** Furniture
  that already existed before the group arrived and remains after it
  leaves matches the site's own permanence, rather than adding one
  more transient thing to set up.
- **A drinking water fountain.**
- **A portable toilet, if none exists on site, during summer.**
- **Car parking nearby.**

None of this is philosophy dressed up as logistics — it's plain
duty-of-care and practical site selection, the same register as the
first aid kit already listed below.

## Materials checklist

**Core — needed for all four acts:**
- A truck, van, or similar vehicle — the centerpiece and the only fixed prop
- Mats or blankets, laid out for L'Usato
- Plinths or easels for L'Arte (the Gallery)
- A blackboard, permanently painted with the heading **OFFERTE E FAVORI**,
  plus chalk
- A bell, for the Remembrancer to mark the end of Act Three
- A chiller tap or cooler, and glasses, for Il Brindisi
- Pen and paper, for the Remembrancer's own chronicle page

**Role-specific props — see 07-staging-la-prova.md, "Objects mark
transitions," for why these are objects and never costumes:**
- The Remembrancer's cap, embroidered with a single "R," passed from
  Remembrancer to Remembrancer
- The Barista's apron
- The Trasformatore's shared tool bag — kept simple: basic tools plus a
  small selection of useful odds and ends, one bag for all Trasformatori
  that day, passed hand to hand between turns at the bench
- The chain object's own permanent plinth, separate from the Gallery's
  ordinary pedestals, plus a starting object to place on it — see
  chain-barter.md and roles/il-custode.md for the full mechanic

**Optional, entirely dependent on who shows up or where you are:**
- A simple maker tool at the Transformation Station (a hot glue gun is
  enough)
- A musical instrument, if a Suonatore happens to be present — no assigned
  kit, whatever they bring
- Rosemary or myrtle branches, only if that day's site already has an open
  fire or barbecue going — see 07-staging-la-prova.md, "Scent, when there's
  a fire"
- Three or four juggling balls, a small table, and a simple hat or
  waistcoat, if a Panem et Circenses rehearsal is running that day — see
  panem-et-circenses.md

## Timing, in brief

There's no fixed schedule, and the FAQ's "as long as it takes" is accurate,
not evasive — but a first-time organizer still needs something to plan
around. Rough shape, based on BosaNode's own working assumptions: Act One
runs roughly as long as the Trasformatori's turns at the bench take (around
two hours for four artists at thirty minutes each, with unhurried gaps
between turns); the day as a whole, Lo Spuntino through Il Brindisi, is
comfortably an afternoon-into-evening's worth of time. For the full
logistics — booking, load-out, how a truck's manifest actually gets
assembled — see 07-timeline-la-prova.md (English) or
07-cronologia-la-prova.md (Italian), which cover this in real depth.

## Sample promises, beyond the one example already in the script

The script gives one worked example — a basket of lemons, by the next
prova. A few more, to show the actual range:

- *Giulia offre a Marco: due ore di aiuto a riparare la sua bicicletta,
  entro la prossima prova.* (Giulia offers Marco: two hours helping repair
  his bicycle, by the next prova.)
- *Antonio offre a Sara: un vaso di miele delle sue api, quando sarà
  pronto.* (Antonio offers Sara: a jar of his own honey, whenever it's
  ready — no fixed date, just before the next prova arrives.)
- *Elena offre a chiunque ne abbia bisogno: lezioni di chitarra per un
  pomeriggio.* (Elena offers whoever needs it: an afternoon of guitar
  lessons.) — a promise doesn't have to name someone already known; it can
  simply be open to whoever takes it up.

## Where the rest of this already lives

Rather than repeating it here, these questions already have real answers
elsewhere in the project:

- **Is this legal?** — bosataz-fiscal.md, understanding-permuta.md, and
  understanding-donazione.md cover the permuta and donazione basis for
  Acts One and Two in full legal depth, including the conguaglio and
  abitualità risks worth knowing about.
- **What does the chain object actually involve?** — chain-barter.md (the
  concept and its history) and roles/il-custode.md (the role it creates).
- **What does each role actually need to know how to do?** —
  roles/il-trasformatore.md, roles/la-sarta.md, roles/il-commerciante.md,
  roles/remembrancer.md, and roles/il-suonatore.md each cover their own
  role's skills and boundaries.
- **What's the full staging picture?** — 07-staging-la-prova.md, including
  the three-tracks model (Food, Theatre, Music) and how transitions are
  signalled without announcements.

## What this pack deliberately doesn't cover

Permits, insurance, food safety, and formal event logistics are real
questions for a different stage — once a Node is ready to move from
ordinary rehearsals toward a formal, publicized event. They're not
addressed here on purpose.
