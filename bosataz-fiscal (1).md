# BosaTAZ — Fiscal

*Part of the TAZ folder. Bosa's own worked instance of the fiscal
boundary named in bosataz-boundaries.md — grounded specifically in
Italian civil law, not a claim about any other Node's legal
footing. This is a working reference, not legal advice: before
anything here is relied on for a formal ZAT application or a PPS
proposal, it needs review by someone actually qualified in Italian
law, not just a document that's been reasoned through carefully.*

## The legal basis

Two things move value at BosaTAZ, and Italian law already has a
name and a settled status for both, with no need for anyone's
permission:

- **Permuta** — barter, the direct exchange of one thing for
  another, with no money involved. This covers the Gallery: art for
  art, object for object.
- **Donazione** — gift, given freely, with nothing expected in
  return. This covers L'Usato, where nothing is asked for what's
  taken.

Neither requires registration, a licence, or a gatekeeper's
approval to be valid. A permuta or a donazione is legally complete
the instant it happens — the same property that makes a promise in
Act Three valid the moment it's signed and posted, not when it's
later fulfilled. Nothing that happens at BosaTAZ needs anyone's
permission to already be real.

## The one rule that has to hold: no conguaglio

Permuta only keeps its status as barter — not a sale — if no cash
changes hands alongside the object. The moment a conguaglio (a cash
top-up) enters an exchange, Italian law starts treating it as a
partial vendita, a sale, with all the tax and reporting obligations
that follow.

This is why "no money crosses the boundary" is written into
BosaTAZ's design as a hard rule, not a preference: the Gallery's
zero-money principle isn't an aesthetic choice, it's the specific
legal condition permuta's status depends on. There's no soft
version of this rule — a small top-up isn't a small exception, it's
the whole legal footing changing underneath the exchange.

## The risk that scale creates: abitualità

Permuta and donazione are protected at the scale they're naturally
used at — occasional, non-recurring exchange between people who
aren't running it as a business. Regular, patterned exchange between
the same parties risks abitualità: reclassification as an undeclared
commercial activity, which loses the protection permuta and
donazione otherwise carry.

This is where BosaTAZ's temporary, non-persistent design is doing
real legal work, not just matching the project's aesthetic
commitments. A zone that appears once a rehearsal and dissolves
completely — no standing relay, no accumulating balance between the
same two people, nothing that persists between events — is close to
structurally immune to this risk, because "occasional, not repeated
between the same parties" is close to the actual legal test. The
truck packing down isn't just satisfying. It's the mechanism keeping
the whole arrangement legal.

## What the record is actually for

None of this needs a ledger to be legally valid — a permuta or
donazione is complete the instant it happens, witnessed or not. The
Remembrancer's chalk, BosaTAZ's seven layers of digital witnessing,
and the photograph taken before the board comes down aren't
compliance instruments. Nothing here is required by law.

What they're actually for is the promisee's side of Act Three,
which has no institutional backstop by design — no scribe, no
arbiter, no court standing behind a promise. The record exists so
someone a promise was made to has something to point to socially if
it goes unkept, not so an official has something to inspect. This
is worth being precise about, because it cuts the other way too: if
this record were ever treated as a formal ledger of taxable
transactions, that would undermine the very informality the fiscal
protection depends on. The record proves a promise was witnessed.
It was never meant to prove a transaction occurred in any sense the
tax code would recognize — there isn't one to prove.

## The staged path, and what each stage actually needs

- **Ordinary Prova** — informal, run as often as needed, no
  application, no formal exposure beyond the underlying permuta and
  donazione protections already described above holding on their
  own merits.
- **ZAT** — a single, formal, publicized event, requiring an actual
  application to the Comune di Bosa. This is the first point where
  the reasoning above needs to hold up in front of someone who isn't
  inclined to take it on faith. The application's job is small and
  specific: permission for one temporary artistic-theatrical event,
  not a case for anything beyond it.
- **PPS** — only pursued after a ZAT has already run and succeeded.
  A different, later, larger proposal, made in collaboration with
  other local cultural groups, with its own legal grounding still to
  be worked out separately from what's written here.

## What this document doesn't cover

This file is scoped to the fiscal boundary as it governs exchange
inside BosaTAZ itself — permuta, donazione, and the conditions that
keep both protected. It doesn't cover the ZAT application's own
administrative requirements, the eventual PPS proposal's legal
structure, or anything to do with Bosa™'s separate cooperative
corporate work, which runs on an entirely different legal register
and is deliberately kept unconnected to this one.
