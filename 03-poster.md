# SLOW THEATRE

*A performance philosophy, grown from Slow Food.*

---

## WHY & WHAT

- Fast theatre is finished, ticketed, and could happen anywhere. Slow Theatre is unfinished, open, and belongs to this place.
- **Good.** Judged by what it feels like to be in the room — not by size, polish, or press.
- **Clean.** No sponsor, no ticket, no gatekeeper deciding who's allowed in.
- **Fair.** No fixed line between performer and audience. Everyone present has equal standing.
- Grown patiently, locally, and slowly — like the soil, not the season.

## HOW

- No experience needed. No audition. You take part by showing up.
- Watching counts as taking part — you're free to step forward at any point, or not at all.
- Each gathering is one rehearsal, not a final version. Nothing needs to be finished tonight.
- Bring only what feels honest to bring — an object, a story, a question, or just yourself.

## WHEN

- No fixed season. Dates and places are shared as they're set.
- Join the group to find out when and where — and to bring your own.
